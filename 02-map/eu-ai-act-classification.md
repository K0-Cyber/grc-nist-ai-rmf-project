# EU AI Act Classification

## Purpose

This document classifies EugAI under the EU AI Act risk-based framework.

The purpose is to determine whether EugAI is prohibited, high risk, limited risk, or minimal risk, and to identify the compliance obligations that apply.

## System Being Classified

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| System Name | EugAI Customer Service Chatbot |
| Organisation | EugTech Solutions Ltd |
| System Type | Conversational AI chatbot |
| Use Case | Customer service support |
| Users | Client employees using EugTech support portals |
| Output Type | Natural language text responses |
| Deployment Region | UK and EU client environment |

## EU AI Act Risk Tiers

| Risk Tier | Description | EugAI Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Unacceptable Risk | AI practices banned under the EU AI Act | Not applicable |
| High Risk | AI systems used in regulated high-impact areas such as employment, education, credit, biometric identification, or essential services | Not applicable in current deployment |
| Limited Risk | AI systems with transparency obligations, including chatbots interacting with humans | Applicable |
| Minimal Risk | AI systems with no mandatory obligations | Not applicable because EugAI interacts with humans |

## Step 1: Prohibited Practices Check

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does EugAI use subliminal techniques? | No |
| Does EugAI exploit vulnerabilities of users? | No |
| Does EugAI perform social scoring? | No |
| Does EugAI perform real-time remote biometric identification in public spaces? | No |
| Does EugAI manipulate users in a harmful way? | No |

**Result:** EugAI is not a prohibited AI system.

## Step 2: High Risk Check

| High Risk Area | Applies to EugAI? | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Employment or HR decisions | No | EugAI is not used for hiring, promotion, dismissal, or task allocation |
| Education or vocational training | No | EugAI is not used to assess students or control access to education |
| Critical infrastructure | No | EugAI does not manage critical infrastructure |
| Law enforcement | No | EugAI is not used by law enforcement |
| Migration, asylum, or border control | No | EugAI is not used for immigration decisions |
| Administration of justice | No | EugAI is not used in legal decision-making |
| Biometric identification | No | EugAI does not process biometric data |
| Access to essential services | No | EugAI does not decide access to credit, benefits, emergency services, or insurance |

**Result:** EugAI is not High Risk in its current deployment.

## Step 3: Limited Risk Check

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does EugAI interact with natural persons? | Yes |
| Is EugAI a chatbot or virtual assistant? | Yes |
| Does EugAI generate text responses to users? | Yes |
| Do users need to know they are interacting with AI? | Yes |

**Result:** EugAI is a Limited Risk AI system.

## Final Classification

| Classification | Result |
|---|---|
| Final EU AI Act classification | Limited Risk |
| Main obligation | Transparency |
| Required control | Users must be informed they are interacting with AI |
| High Risk obligations | Not applicable in current deployment |
| Reclassification trigger | Use in employment, credit, education, essential services, biometric, law enforcement, or other high-impact areas |

## Transparency Requirement

Because EugAI is a chatbot interacting with humans, users must be informed clearly that they are interacting with an AI system.

This should be shown:

- At the beginning of the conversation
- In clear and understandable language
- Before the user relies on the AI response
- With an option to contact a human support agent

## Reclassification Warning

EugAI could become High Risk if it is later used for:

- Recruitment or employee screening
- Credit or insurance eligibility
- Education access or assessment
- Access to essential public or private services
- Biometric identification or categorisation
- Any decision that significantly affects individual rights

Before any High Risk use, EugTech would need a full High Risk compliance assessment.

## Summary

EugAI is currently classified as a Limited Risk AI system under the EU AI Act because it is a conversational chatbot that interacts with humans.

The main compliance requirement is transparency. Users must know they are speaking to AI and must have access to human support where needed.
