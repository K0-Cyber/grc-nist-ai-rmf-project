# AI Risk Appetite Statement

## Purpose

This document defines EugTech Solutions Ltd's risk appetite for the use of EugAI, a simulated AI customer service chatbot.

The purpose of this statement is to define which AI risks are acceptable, which require treatment, and which are outside the organisation's tolerance.

## Risk Appetite Summary

EugTech Solutions Ltd has a low appetite for AI risks that could cause harm to users, breach privacy obligations, mislead customers, or damage trust.

The organisation accepts limited and controlled use of AI for low-impact customer support activities where human escalation and monitoring controls are in place.

## Acceptable AI Use

EugAI may be used for:

- First-line customer service queries
- Product information retrieval
- Troubleshooting guidance
- Ticket triage
- Routing customers to human agents
- Providing answers from approved knowledge base content

## Unacceptable AI Use

EugAI must not be used for:

- Medical advice
- Legal advice
- Financial advice
- Employment decisions
- Credit decisions
- Access to essential services
- Automated decisions affecting individual rights
- Processing sensitive personal data without approval
- Generating discriminatory, harmful, or misleading content

## AI Risk Appetite by Category

| Risk Category | Risk Appetite | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Security | Low | Prompt injection, jailbreaks, and data extraction attempts must be actively controlled |
| Privacy | Low | Personal data exposure could create legal and reputational harm |
| Fairness and Bias | Low | Systematic lower-quality service for user groups is not acceptable |
| Reliability | Medium | Minor inaccuracies may occur, but high-impact errors must be escalated |
| Transparency | Low | Users must always know they are interacting with AI |
| Accountability | Low | Human ownership must exist for AI decisions and failures |
| Availability | Medium | Temporary service disruption is tolerable if human support remains available |

## Risk Treatment Expectations

| Risk Level | Required Action |
|---|---|
| High | Treat within 30 days and report to leadership |
| Medium | Treat within 90 days and monitor regularly |
| Low | Accept with documented controls and periodic review |

## Human Oversight Requirements

Human review is required when:

- EugAI produces a low-confidence response
- A user asks about a sensitive topic
- A query involves personal data
- A query appears to relate to medical, legal, financial, or employment matters
- The user requests escalation
- The AI response may affect customer rights, obligations, or access to services

## Review Cycle

This risk appetite statement will be reviewed:

- Annually
- After a serious AI incident
- After a major change to EugAI
- When new AI regulatory obligations apply
- When EugAI is proposed for a higher-risk use case
