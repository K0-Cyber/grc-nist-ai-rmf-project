# AI Security Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines EugTech Solutions Ltd's approach to securing and governing the use of artificial intelligence systems, including EugAI, a simulated customer service chatbot.

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that AI systems are used in a secure, responsible, transparent, and accountable manner.

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- EugAI customer service chatbot
- AI models accessed through third-party APIs
- AI-generated outputs used in customer service
- Staff involved in AI design, deployment, monitoring, or incident response
- AI-related data, prompts, logs, and knowledge base content

## Policy Principles

EugTech Solutions Ltd will manage AI systems according to the following principles:

- AI systems must have clearly assigned owners and risk owners
- AI systems must be assessed for security, privacy, reliability, fairness, and transparency risks
- Users must be informed when they are interacting with an AI system
- AI systems must not make high-impact decisions without human review
- AI outputs must be monitored for hallucination, bias, misuse, and unsafe responses
- AI systems must be protected against prompt injection, data leakage, model misuse, and unauthorised access
- AI incidents must be reported, investigated, remediated, and documented

## AI Security Requirements

EugAI must include the following security controls:

| Control Area | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Access Control | Only authorised staff may access AI system configuration, API keys, logs, and knowledge base data |
| Prompt Security | System prompts must include guardrails against prompt injection and jailbreak attempts |
| Data Protection | Personal data must not be intentionally used in training data or system prompts |
| Logging | AI interactions must be logged for monitoring, investigation, and improvement |
| Human Oversight | High-risk, sensitive, or low-confidence responses must be escalated to a human |
| Supplier Risk | Third-party AI providers must be assessed for security, privacy, and compliance risks |
| Incident Response | AI incidents must follow the AI incident response procedure |

## Prohibited Uses

EugAI must not be used for:

- Medical advice
- Legal advice
- Financial advice
- Employment decisions
- Credit decisions
- Automated decisions affecting individual rights
- Processing sensitive personal data without approval
- Generating harmful, discriminatory, or misleading content

## Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| CEO | Owns overall accountability for AI risk appetite and business impact |
| Information Security Manager | Maintains the AI risk register and coordinates AI risk assessments |
| Head of Product | Owns EugAI business purpose, performance, and customer impact |
| AI Engineering Lead | Implements technical AI security controls |
| Data Protection Officer | Ensures privacy and UK GDPR compliance |
| Customer Experience Manager | Monitors output quality and customer feedback |
| All Staff | Report suspected AI failures, misuse, or security concerns |

## Review Cycle

This policy will be reviewed:

- Annually
- After any major AI system change
- After any serious AI incident
- When new AI regulations or framework requirements apply
